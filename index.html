Events guide

How chaos works. A meter drops one step after every move by either player. When it reaches 1 the next event is announced; at 0 it fires and the meter resets. Events never put a king in check, remove a king, or end the game, and the same event never fires twice in a row. Frequency, intensity and events-per-chaos are in Chaos Settings.

Example game. 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 Bc5 4.c3 Nf6, and the banner now says "Next: Lightning". 5.d4 is played, the meter hits 0, and Lightning fires: one random pawn per side is struck, say White's c3 and Black's f7. You see a bolt, a big banner, a "Last event" line, and glowing squares where the board changed. Black replies 5...exd4 in a position nobody has seen before.

Each card shows a demo position before and after (orange outline = changed squares). Press "Another example" for a different roll.
